Appointment Planner

Chips show batch specification dates (Spec:) and clinic dates (Clinic:). Click a clinic chip to edit it. Click a batch chip to focus it — that shades its "runway" (how long it takes to get everyone in the batch an appointment, at 50 appointments per weekday) and shows its linked clinics below. The arrows slide the 4-month window one month at a time.

Data

All data is stored in this browser only (via localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere. Export regularly to back up your work or move it to another computer or browser.

Publish for others to see

To let others viewing this site see your data: click Export JSON above, rename the downloaded file to data.json, and add/replace it in the repo next to index.html (commit & push, or use GitHub's "Upload files"). A visitor opening the site for the first time in their browser will load it automatically; anyone can also pull a fresh copy at any time with the button below.

This isn't live — it's a manual snapshot. Once a browser has its own data (from editing, importing, or loading the published copy once), it keeps using that until "Reload published data" is clicked again.
